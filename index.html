About

Built by someone who used the gear first

Moonmang imports and distributes IT hardware in Korea. Our founder spent years in 3D and motion graphics work, where keyboards, mice and monitors were the tools of the trade, and then went on to sell IT peripherals on Korean open markets — writing the product pages and handling the returns that followed.

That is why we would rather carry few brands and make their Korean numbers real. Adding one more name to a list is easy. Watching what actually happens to that brand in Korea is not.

Founder's Note

I spent most of my working days at a desk, doing 3D and motion graphics. I learned the hard way that a keyboard which does not fit your hand slows down the whole day, and that is where I first understood why good hardware is worth what it costs.

After that I sold IT peripherals online myself. That is when I found out that listing a product is the short part. Answering questions, processing returns, reading reviews and looking at the price again — that part does not stop.

When I started Moonmang I decided one thing: not to collect brands. I will take on only as many as I can follow all the way through in Korea. If we end up working together, I will tell you up front how far I can go and where I cannot.

Jungung Moon Founder, Moonmang

Korean Compliance

What Korea asks of IT hardware

Two mice can need entirely different paperwork depending on whether they are wired, wireless or carry a battery. These are the items we check before an IT product is brought in.

Radio Conformity (RRA)전파법 적합성평가

Wireless keyboards and mice, Bluetooth devices and anything shipping with a 2.4GHz dongle require conformity assessment from the National Radio Research Agency. We first check whether the module already holds certification.

Electrical Safety (KC)전기용품 KC 안전확인

Adapters, chargers and other mains-powered components are checked against Korea's electrical appliance safety rules, and taken through safety confirmation where required.

Built-in Batteries배터리 내장 제품

Products with lithium cells can fall under separate safety requirements and shipping rules, so we review cell specifications and test reports in advance.

Korean Labelling한글 표시사항

Importer, country of manufacture, model name and certification numbers must appear in Korean on the product and packaging. We propose a layout that does not fight your packaging design.

Customs & Duties수입 통관·관세

Duty follows HS classification, and where a trade agreement applies we check whether a certificate of origin can bring a preferential rate.

Recycling Obligation (EPR)전기·전자제품 재활용(EPR)

Depending on the product type, take-back and recycling obligations for waste electrical goods may apply. We check whether they do.

The list above is an overview. What actually applies, and what it costs, depends on the specification and the bill of materials — we confirm it product by product once we have your spec sheet.
